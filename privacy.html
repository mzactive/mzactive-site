This notice explains how MZ Active handles Coach/Pacer applications and booking requests on mzactive.com. By submitting a form, you consent to the described use of the information you provide. Contact mzactive@yahoo.com to ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your information.

Information and purpose · المعلومات والغرض

Provider applications may include name, account email, Qatar service area, languages, specialties, experience, biography, achievements, personal bests, comfortable pace, indicative rates, availability and a public Strava/Garmin link. MZ Active uses these details to create and review a profile. Only profiles approved by an administrator are shown publicly. Profile photos are not accepted by the current form.

Booking requests include name, WhatsApp number, Qatar location, preferred date, session type, distance and target pace. We use them to review availability and arrange the requested session. After a request is saved, your browser opens WhatsApp with the request details so you can coordinate manually with MZ Active and receive Fawran instructions. A request is not a confirmed booking; the website does not process payments or store card or bank credentials.

Service providers and location · مقدمو الخدمة ومكان البيانات

The marketplace API and its separate database run on Cloudflare Workers/D1; the database is in the APAC region, not Qatar. Booking details are sent to WhatsApp (Meta) only in the handoff initiated after a successful save. Approved profile details are public on mzactive.com. Do not include health records, identity documents or other sensitive information in profile or booking text.

Retention · مدة الاحتفاظ

Booking requests and moderation records are retained for up to 12 months after the request is closed, then deleted. Provider account/profile details are retained while the account is active; inactive accounts are deleted after 24 months, or earlier on a verified deletion request, except where records must be retained to resolve a dispute or comply with law.

Questions or access, correction and deletion requests: mzactive@yahoo.com. This notice is operational information and does not claim a legal compliance certification.
